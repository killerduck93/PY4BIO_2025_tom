🧬 Caspases Cleavage Site Predictor

About: This tool predicts potential cleavage sites by caspases in protein sequences.

Caspases are enzymes involved in vital cellular processes and play a prominent role in apoptosis and inflammation.

Default IDs: O00238, P17405, Q01814, Q9H3R0, Q9HCP0

ℹ️ Information

Caspases Supported:

  • Caspase-1 through Caspase-10
  • Each caspase recognizes specific 8-amino acid motifs
  • Cleavage occurs between positions P1 and P1' (between 4th and 5th amino acid)

Output includes:

  • Accession ID and Protein Name
  • Caspase Type
  • 8-character cleavage site sequence
  • Position in protein sequence
  • Total protein length
